Performance measures

Amdahl's law

speedup  S = ToldTnew = 1(1 − f) + fsupper limit  Smax = 11 − f

f = fraction of the time that can be sped up, s = speedup of that fraction. The part that cannot be improved limits the total gain.

2.3

Integer addition and subtraction

  • Add bit by bit with carry: 1 + 1 = 10, 1 + 1 + 1 = 11.
  • Subtraction A − B = A + (2's complement of B); ignore the carry out.
  • Overflow (2's complement): adding two numbers of the same sign gives a result of the opposite sign; equivalently carry into the sign bit ≠ carry out of it.
  • Hardware: a ripple-carry adder with XOR gates on B and carry-in = 1 for subtraction.

2.4

Booth's multiplication algorithm

Multiplies signed 2's complement numbers. Registers: A = 0, Q = multiplier, Q−1 = 0, M = multiplicand; repeat n times:

  • Look at Q0 Q−1: 10 → A = A − M; 01 → A = A + M; 00 or 11 → no operation.
  • Arithmetic shift right the combined A, Q, Q−1 (sign bit of A is kept).
  • After n steps the 2n-bit product is in A Q.

2.5

Restoring division

Unsigned n-bit division. A = 0, Q = dividend, M = divisor; repeat n times:

  • Shift A Q left one bit.
  • A = A − M. If A < 0: set Q0 = 0 and restore A = A + M. Otherwise set Q0 = 1.
  • End: Q = quotient, A = remainder.
  • Non-restoring division avoids the restore step by adding M in the next cycle instead.

Floating-point operations

  • Add / subtract: align the exponents (shift the smaller mantissa right), add or subtract the mantissas, normalise, round, check overflow / underflow.
  • Multiply: add the exponents (subtract one bias), multiply the mantissas, XOR the signs, normalise, round.
  • Divide: subtract the exponents (add the bias back), divide the mantissas, normalise.
  • Rounding modes: nearest-even (default), toward 0, toward +∞, toward −∞.
