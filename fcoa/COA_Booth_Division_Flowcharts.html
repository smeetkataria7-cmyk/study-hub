Booth's Multiplication Algorithm: Flowchart

n = number of bits. M = multiplicand, Q = multiplier, A = accumulator, Q₋₁ = extra bit to the right of Q. The shift keeps the sign bit of A (arithmetic shift). The 2n-bit product is in A and Q.

Booth's: worked example 7 × 3 (n = 4)

M = 0111, Q = 0011. Product = 00010101 = 21.

  2. Then arithmetic shift right A, Q, Q₋₁, and decrement Count.

Restoring Division Algorithm: Flowchart

n = number of bits. M = divisor, Q = dividend (n bits), A = 0 at the start. Applies to unsigned (positive) numbers. For signed numbers, divide the magnitudes and set the signs afterwards. The quotient is in Q and the remainder in A.

M = 0011, Q = 0111. Quotient = 2 (0010), remainder = 1.
